Data Governance Policy

Section: Classification
Data is classified Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer identifiers, authentication data, and payment records are Restricted.

Section: Retention
Support tickets are retained for three years. Security audit logs are retained for seven years. Expired records are deleted through the approved disposal workflow.

Section: Access review
Owners review access to Restricted data quarterly. Removed users must lose access within four hours of the approved removal request.
