Employee Onboarding Handbook

Section: First day
New employees report at 9:30 AM, present government identification, collect a badge, and attend the security briefing before receiving system access.

Section: Mandatory training
Security awareness and data privacy training must be completed within five business days. Managers receive an alert when either course is overdue.

Section: Equipment
Remote employees receive a laptop and headset by courier. Damage must be reported within 24 hours of delivery with photographs of the packaging.
