Machine Learning Course Guide

Section: Assessment
The final grade is 30 percent assignments, 20 percent quizzes, 20 percent midterm, and 30 percent project. A total score of 50 percent is required to pass.

Section: Late work
Assignments lose 10 percent per calendar day for up to three days. After three days, submissions are accepted only with an approved extension.

Section: Project
Projects are completed in teams of three or four. The proposal is due in week 5, checkpoint in week 9, and presentation in week 13.
