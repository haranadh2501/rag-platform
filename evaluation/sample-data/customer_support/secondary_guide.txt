NovaHub Troubleshooting

Section: Factory reset
To reset NovaHub, hold the rear reset button for 10 seconds until the status light flashes amber. The reset removes local settings but does not cancel the subscription.

Section: Offline status
If NovaHub shows offline, verify power, restart the router, and move the device within eight metres of the access point. If still offline, collect the diagnostic code.

Section: Escalation
Escalate when three resets fail, the device overheats, or a diagnostic code begins with HW-. Overheating devices must be unplugged immediately.
