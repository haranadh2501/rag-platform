Customer Support Policy

Section: Warranty
The NovaHub device has a two-year limited hardware warranty from the purchase date. Accidental damage and unauthorized repairs are excluded.

Section: Returns
Unopened products may be returned within 30 calendar days. Opened defective products require a support case number and prepaid return label.

Section: Replacement SLA
Approved in-warranty replacements ship within two business days. Expedited shipping is available only for premium support customers.
