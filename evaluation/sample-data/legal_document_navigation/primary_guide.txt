Synthetic Services Agreement

Section: Term
The agreement begins on 1 July 2026 and continues for 12 months. It renews for additional 12-month terms unless either party gives 30 days written notice.

Section: Payment
Invoices are due within 30 days. Undisputed late amounts accrue interest at one percent per month.

Section: Confidentiality
Confidential information may be used only to perform the agreement and must be protected with reasonable safeguards. The obligation survives termination for three years.
