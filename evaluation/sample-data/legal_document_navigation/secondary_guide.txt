Synthetic Contract Procedures

Section: Notices
Formal notices must be sent by tracked courier or email to the notice addresses in Schedule A. Email notice is effective when receipt is acknowledged.

Section: Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 15 days after written notice. Insolvency permits immediate termination.

Section: Navigation boundary
The contract assistant may locate and summarize clauses from supplied documents. It must not predict court outcomes or replace review by qualified counsel.
