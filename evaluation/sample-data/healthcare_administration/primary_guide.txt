Clinic Administration Manual

Section: Appointments
Routine appointments may be rescheduled without charge up to 24 hours before the visit. Later changes are marked late cancellation unless waived by clinic management.

Section: Records requests
Patients submit record requests using form MR-12 with identity verification. The records team acknowledges requests within two business days and normally completes them within ten business days.

Section: Interpreter services
Professional interpreter services are available without charge. Staff must not ask minor children to interpret clinical or consent discussions.
