Billing and Privacy Procedures

Section: Billing questions
Billing disputes require the invoice number, service date, disputed line item, and contact details. Clinical staff must not promise that a charge will be removed.

Section: Privacy incidents
Misdirected records or unauthorized chart access must be reported to the Privacy Office immediately. Staff should preserve evidence and avoid contacting the unintended recipient without instructions.

Section: Administrative boundary
Administrative staff may explain scheduling, records, billing, and privacy procedures. They must not diagnose conditions, interpret test results, or recommend treatment.
